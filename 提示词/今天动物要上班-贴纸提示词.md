# 《今天动物要上班！》手帐贴纸提示词

网页右侧有 4 张贴纸，每张 9 枚，一共 36 枚。
生成后切成单枚**透明背景 PNG**，命名为 `s01.png` … `s36.png`，放进 `output/stickers/`，刷新网页即可。
**白色描边、光泽、贴上去的阴影都由网页自动加**（文字现在画在贴纸下方，不再用气泡），所以生成时不要画白边。

## 推荐做法：一次生成一整版，再切开

一张 3×3 的图，正好对应一张贴纸（9 枚）。生成后用抠图工具去掉背景，再切成 9 张。

### 通用风格前缀（每版都先粘这一段）· 粗蜡笔手绘风

```
Sticker sheet of 9 separate hand-drawn stickers arranged in a loose 3x3 grid with generous spacing,
drawn like a child's crayon / oil-pastel doodle on paper: very thick, wobbly, uneven black crayon outlines
with rough waxy grainy edges, line weight swelling and thinning, slightly broken strokes, lines that overlap
or don't fully close, imperfect shaky hand, NOT vector, NOT clean digital lines.
Colors are rough crayon scribble fills: visible stroke texture, paper grain showing through,
color going slightly outside the outline, patchy uneven coverage, warm limited palette
(mustard yellow, orange, caramel brown, chocolate brown, soft pink, white).
Original chubby cats (orange tabby, calico, grey, black-and-white tuxedo), big simple head, tiny body,
small round dot eyes or big googly eyes, goofy funny expressions, open mouths with pink tongue.
Each sticker = one cat doing one action + a short Chinese word hand-lettered below it
in chunky wobbly black crayon handwriting (no speech bubble).
Pure flat white background, no white sticker border, no drop shadow, no gradient, no extra text,
every sticker fully separated.
```

> 想让手绘感更强，可以把上面那张狗狗蜡笔图作为参考图一起上传，并加一句：
> `match the crayon line texture and scribble coloring of the reference image, but draw original cats, do not copy the dogs`

## 小技巧
- **文字写错：** GPT 生成的中文偶尔会出错。可以改成 `no text`，只画猫；网页之后可以自动在下面写字，需要的话告诉我。
- **线条还不够糙：** 再加 `extra thick chunky crayon strokes, more texture, more wobbly`。
